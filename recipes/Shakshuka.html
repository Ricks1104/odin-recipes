Shakshuka

Shakshuka is a vibrant North African and Middle Eastern dish of eggs gently poached in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce. It’s popular across Tunisia, Israel, and much of the region, and it’s become a brunch favorite worldwide. It’s warm, smoky, and satisfying, and it’s typically served straight from the pan with crusty bread for scooping. It’s also naturally vegetarian, budget-friendly, and made in a single skillet.

The recipe starts with a base of onions, bell peppers, and garlic softened in olive oil, then seasoned with cumin, paprika, and a pinch of chili for warmth. Crushed tomatoes are added and simmered until the sauce thickens enough to hold small wells. Eggs are cracked into these wells and cooked gently, covered, until the whites set but the yolks stay runny. The finishing touches make the dish: a scattering of crumbled feta, fresh parsley or cilantro, and a drizzle of olive oil. It comes together in about 30 minutes and easily scales up for a crowd by using a larger pan.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet or smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp chili flakes or cayenne (adjust to taste)
  • 1 can (about 800 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 5–6 large eggs
  • 50 g feta cheese, crumbled
  • Fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped
  • Crusty bread or pita, for serving

Below are the steps to execute the ingredients into a beautiful made and delicious dish

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and bell pepper, and cook for 6–8 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika, and chili flakes, and stir for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, then add the crushed tomatoes. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Simmer uncovered for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.
  5. Use a spoon to make 5–6 small wells in the sauce, and crack an egg into each one.
  6. Cover the skillet and cook on low heat for 5–8 minutes, until the egg whites are set and the yolks are still runny.
  7. Remove from the heat and sprinkle with feta and fresh herbs.
  8. Drizzle with a little olive oil and serve immediately with crusty bread.
